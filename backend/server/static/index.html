Features

Everything a study session needs, kept on one desk

  1. 01

    Lectures, slides and recordings

    Add a PDF or PowerPoint deck, or an MP3 or MP4 recording. Notiv renders every page, transcribes the audio with timestamps and keeps your original file.

  2. 02

    Handwritten notes, read properly

    Scanned or handwritten pages are read from the page itself. Anything that can't be read reliably is flagged, never quietly passed off as your notes.

  3. 03

    Summaries and concept maps

    An overview, key takeaways and topic-by-topic summaries, plus a map of how the ideas connect — each part citing where it came from.

  4. 04

    Flashcards and quizzes

    Questions that test understanding rather than copy-paste, with the answer and its source one tap away. Add your own to a question bank, too.

  5. 05

    Notebooks and a canvas

    Gather a course in one notebook, write notes beside your lectures, and lay out concepts on a canvas. Share a read-only link when you want to.

  6. 06

    A timer and a small companion

    A focus timer that keeps true time in background tabs, and a cat on your desk that opens Ask, the timer and practice — or naps out of the way.

How it works

From a file to a study session in four steps

  1. 1

    Upload

    Add PDF, PPTX, MP3 or MP4 files. Your original files are kept as they are.

  2. 2

    Let it process

    Watch the progress as pages are read and audio is transcribed. If something fails, you're told why and can retry.

  3. 3

    Study

    Read the summary, follow the concept map, play the recording and open the exact source of anything.

  4. 4

    Ask and practise

    Question the material, then test yourself with flashcards, quizzes and your own question bank.

FAQs

Questions, answered

Something else? The help page covers uploads and answers in more detail.

Which files can I upload?

PDF and PowerPoint (PPTX) for slides and documents, and MP3 and MP4 for recordings. Other formats are not accepted.

Can it read my handwritten notes?

Yes, when the server has an image-reading AI service set up: handwritten or scanned pages are read from the page image. Pages that can't be read reliably are flagged and left out, so nothing unreadable is presented as your notes.

Where do answers come from?

From the lecture you ask about. Each answer links to its source. If the lecture doesn't contain the answer, Notiv says so, and you can optionally ask for a separately labelled general-knowledge explanation.

Do summaries, flashcards and quizzes need setup?

They're written by an AI service that the server must be configured with. Without one, you still get verbatim excerpts from your material, your own question bank and every other feature.

Can I share a notebook?

You can create a read-only link for a notebook, set it to expire, and revoke it at any time.

Does the timer keep running if I switch tabs?

Yes. It counts to a fixed end time, so it stays accurate when the tab is in the background.
