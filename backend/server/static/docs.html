Documentation

How Notiv works, in the order you'll meet it.

Supported files

PDF and PowerPoint (PPTX) for slides and documents. MP3 and MP4 for recordings. Other formats are not accepted.

The workflow

  1. Upload from the Library or inside a notebook.
  2. Processing shows progress for each file. A failed file can be retried; your original is kept.
  3. Study by opening a lecture: pages or slides, the recording with timestamps, notes and study material.
  4. Ask a question from the Ask page, a notebook, or the study companion cat. Answers cite their sources.
  5. Practise with your own question bank, or with an AI-generated quiz if your server has an AI service configured.

Focus timer

Open the cat on your desk and choose Focus timer. Set focus and break lengths, start, pause, resume or reset. The timer keeps correct time while the tab is in the background.

Keyboard

Every control is reachable with Tab. Escape closes the cat menu and timer. Enter sends a chat message; Shift+Enter adds a new line.
