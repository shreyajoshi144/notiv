Privacy

A plain-language summary of how this app behaves.

  • Your notebooks, notes, uploads and study progress belong to your account and are not visible to other accounts.
  • Share links show one notebook read-only. They stop working when they expire or you revoke them.
  • Your browser session uses a secure HttpOnly cookie. Notiv's pages load no third-party scripts or fonts.
  • Your focus-timer and companion preferences are stored in your own browser only.
  • If your server administrator enables an external AI service, the text needed to answer a question or build a quiz is sent to that service.

This summary describes the software. The organisation that runs this server is responsible for its own privacy policy and should replace or extend this page.
